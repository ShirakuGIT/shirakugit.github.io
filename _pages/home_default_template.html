    About me

    I am a physicist working in the field of quantum mechanics and quantum electrodynamics. I received my undergraduate degree in physics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) in 1939 and went on to earn my PhD from Princeton University in 1942. After completing my doctoral studies, I worked on the Manhattan Project, where I helped develop the first atomic bombs. After the war, I returned to academia, holding teaching and research positions at Cornell and now at the California Institute of Technology.

    Projects

    • Logistics CoBot: Mobile robot navigation with Nav2 and slam_toolbox, paired with UR5 manipulation via MoveIt2 and ArUco markers.
    • Perception Pipeline: Metric 3D reconstruction of a scene using SAM3D, followed by object localization and grasp generation using FoundationPose and Grasp Sampler.
    • Grasp Sampler: Generates 6-DoF parallel-jaw grasps for any mesh by combining primitive, OBB-face and antipodal samplers.
    • ATOM: Maps Meta Quest 2 controller poses to a robot arm using adaptive inverse kinematics, with support for multiple robots and teleoperation modes.
    • SLAM: Ground-based SLAM using a 3D LiDAR and a suite of other sensors, producing a full map colored by height above ground.

    Demos and videos are on the projects page.
