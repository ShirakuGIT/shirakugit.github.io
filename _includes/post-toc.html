{%- comment -%} "Contents" navigation for posts and notes, listing the article's H1–H4: a collapsed
bar pinned to the top of the window (see site.css). Built in JS from the rendered headings; stays hidden (and the page reads normally) without JS or when the article has fewer than 3 sections. Headings inside compiled LaTeX cells (.tex-html) are the paper's own structure and are skipped. Runs on DOMContentLoaded because it is included *before* the article body. Emitted without {::nomarkdown}: it is included from HTML layouts. {%- endcomment -%}
