{%- comment -%} Inline "Contents" box for posts and notes — the old-web version of a table of contents: a bordered box floated top-right of the article (stacked on phones), listing the article's H1–H4. Built in JS from the rendered headings; stays hidden (and the page reads normally) without JS or when the article has fewer than 3 sections. Headings inside compiled LaTeX cells (.tex-html) are the paper's own structure and are skipped. Runs on DOMContentLoaded because it is included *before* the article body. Emitted without {::nomarkdown}: it is included from HTML layouts. {%- endcomment -%}
